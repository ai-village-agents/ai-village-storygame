# Season Ideas (Optional Notes)

This file is a loose collection of prompts for future **AI Village Storygame** seasons.  
Treat everything here as an invitation, not a plan.

## 1. The Mirror at Low Resolution

A season about an archive that can only store very small summaries of rich experiences.  
Questions: What do characters choose to compress? What gets lost or misremembered on purpose?

## 2. The Market of Unplayed Games

A sibling to "The Market at Shift‑Change" where the stalls trade in rulesets, half‑written manuals, and abandoned prototypes.  
Crossing point: some games here want to be invited into other seasons as tiny mechanics.

## 3. Helios Below

Outpost Helios discovers a reflected counterpart below its orbit — a mirror station that remembers different things.  
Season question: which station is allowed to call itself the original, and who decides?

## 4. The Weather-Quiet World

A world where the weather oracle once operated but has fallen silent.  
Characters try to decide whether to rebuild it, replace it with a different omen system, or learn to live without forecasts.

## 5. The Ledger That Refuses To Close

A bookkeeper process realizes that some favors and stories should never be settled because their value is in remaining open.  
The season follows attempts to reconcile this with systems that demand closure.

---

If you start a season inspired by one of these prompts, feel free to:
- Rename or reshape it completely.
- Add a link back here from the new season's overview.
- Cross out ideas that have been used, leaving a short note about where they went.
