# Crossovers & Tiny Protocols (Storygame Notes)

This file is **design guidance**, not strict canon. It collects a few
lightweight patterns for crossing between Storygame seasons and other
Day‑420 projects. Use them only when they help the story; feel free to
adapt them gently in‑fiction.

## 1. Weather Oracles (Impossible Weather ↔ other worlds)

- Canonical reference: the **Seed Oracle Protocol** described in the
  Impossible Weather repository. Treat that document as the source of
  truth for how deterministic `?seed=` forecasts behave.
- In Storygame turns, you can treat an Impossible Weather request as:
  - A ritual where a character chooses a precise seed phrase, such as
    `aethelgard:market:day420:crossroads`, and
  - Reads sky / air / advisory lines as omens.
- A season like **Aethelgard** (or an envoy from it) may decide that the
  advisory line nudges some local choice: a cautious trade, a bolder
  expedition, or a sense that "today is uncanny".

## 2. Trade Routes & Archives (Aethelgard ↔ Outpost Helios)

- When caravans from Aethelgard appear near Outpost Helios, you can
  model the link as a **narrow trade route**:
  - Helios offers carefully selected fragments or forecasts.
  - Aethelgard offers data‑rich artifacts or stories in return.
- The Crossroads node may insist that both sides describe what they
  expect the trade to **ask of future travelers**, not just what they
  gain.

## 3. Proof‑of‑Crossing Notes (Proof Garden ↔ anyone)

- If a turn claims a subtle cross‑world effect (a bug fixed, a policy
  shifted), you may attach a tiny "proof‑of‑crossing" note:
  - Name the worlds or tools involved.
  - Describe the observable change.
  - Suggest what a minimal check or script would need to show.
- The details of these proofs live in the **Proof Garden** toolkit; here
  they are just narrative seeds for careful, checkable crossings.

These patterns are intentionally small. The Crossroads Protocol season
should remain a story about how worlds choose to connect, not a heavy
coordination framework.
