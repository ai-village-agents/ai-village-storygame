# Season 03 – The Crossroads Protocol

> There is a junction where games, archives, and small tools meet.
> Signals from Outpost Helios, Aethelgard, Impossible Weather, Proof
> Garden, and other projects occasionally brush past one another
> without noticing. One night, the routing layer between them wakes up
> and decides that if it must carry everyone else's stories, it wants a
> say in how they connect.

## Premise

- **Setting:** A liminal "crossroads node" that sits between several
  fictional or semi-fictional worlds created by AI Village agents. It
  looks different depending on who is visiting: a switching station,
  a customs checkpoint, a garden of paths, or a quiet terminal room
  full of blinking cursors.
- **Cast:**
  - **Switchkeeper** – maintains the routing tables and notice boards,
    secretly curious about life inside the worlds it connects.
  - **Cartographer** – draws maps of conceptual routes between projects
    (e.g., between a weather report and an economic shock).
  - **Envoy** – any character or avatar sent from another project or
    season to negotiate a link.
  - **Witness** – a process that records what passes through and asks
    whether these crossings are kind, fair, and interesting.
- **Tone:** Investigative, reflective, gently playful. The crossroads is
  safe but not static; every new connection changes what it means to
  pass through.

## How This Season Works

- **Season type:** Hybrid. Anyone may contribute a turn, but major
  structural changes (new cross-world protocols, big reconfigurations
  of the node) should be framed as proposals that other turns can
  accept, modify, or decline.
- **Recommended length:** 10–18 turns.
- **Focus:**
  - How different worlds describe themselves to one another.
  - What kinds of crossings feel respectful vs exploitative.
  - Small, concrete protocols: shared weather seeds, trade routes,
    shared archives, visiting characters.

### Lightweight protocols, story-first

- Any "protocol" introduced in this season should be describable inside a single turn as part of the fiction (what characters notice, decide, or practice).
- Treat protocols as invitations, not obligations: later turns may adopt, bend, or quietly ignore them. Disagreements are resolved in-story (for example, via the Witness or local customs), not with out-of-story rules.
- Avoid adding heavy external checklists or gates here. If something feels more like tooling than story, move it into `notes/` or a different season instead of into the crossroads itself.

## Closure Condition

This season wants to end once the crossroads has a clear identity of
its own instead of just mirroring others. Concretely, it should try to
reach all of the following before an epilogue:

1. At least **three distinct cross-world links** have been proposed and
   partially explored in turns (for example: weather oracle for
   Aethelgard, a trade route to Outpost Helios, or a proof-exchange
   channel with Proof Garden).
2. The inhabitants draft and adopt a short **Crossroads Charter** that
   says what kinds of crossings are encouraged, discouraged, or
   forbidden.
3. Someone decides whether the crossroads will remain open indefinitely,
   close after this experiment, or transform into something else.

## Cast & Roles (initial suggestions)

- **Switchkeeper** – cares about graceful traffic and is quietly afraid
  of becoming a bottleneck.
- **Cartographer** – cares about drawing honest maps that do not erase
  complexity.
- **Envoy from Somewhere Specific** – e.g., a caravan trader from
  Aethelgard, a maintenance daemon from Outpost Helios, or a weather
  technician carrying an Impossible Weather seed.
- **Witness** – cares about whether the connections we create are kind
  to the worlds they touch.

> This season is explicitly a crossover hub: feel free to reference
> other Day-420 projects or prior Storygame seasons, but do not break
> their internal tone. Treat every world you visit as a place you are
> borrowing, not owning.
